Agriculture in India

India is one of the world's largest agricultural nations, feeding billions and employing nearly half the workforce. Here's what makes it extraordinary.

Diverse Crop Heritage

India grows over 200 commercially important crops including rice, wheat, sugarcane, cotton, pulses, and spices — across vastly different agro-climatic zones.

Water Management

Agriculture uses ~80% of India's fresh water. Smart irrigation can save 30–40% water, dramatically improving sustainability for future generations.

Soil Diversity

India has 8 major soil types — alluvial, black, red, laterite, desert, mountain, forest, and saline — each suited to specific crops and requiring tailored care.

AI Revolution

AI-powered tools can increase farm productivity by 25%, reduce losses by 20%, and help smallholder farmers access the same intelligence as large enterprises.

Climate Challenges

Erratic monsoons, rising temperatures, and extreme weather events cost Indian agriculture ₹50,000+ crore annually. Smart forecasting is critical for resilience.

Government Support

Over 30 central schemes like PM-KISAN, PMFBY, and RKVY provide financial safety nets, insurance, and investment in agricultural modernization.
